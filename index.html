        About the data

        Children living in poverty
        The share of children under 18 living below the federal poverty level, across all the census tracts a community serves. We add up the children in poverty in those tracts and divide by all children whose poverty status is known. Source: Governor’s Office for Children analysis of U.S. Census Bureau American Community Survey 5-year estimates, 2020–2024.
        Why some rates are below 30%
        A community qualifies for ENOUGH if at least one census tract it serves had 30% or more of its children living in poverty in the year it applied. The rate shown here covers all the tracts a community serves, so it can be lower than 30%.
        Funds awarded
        Total ENOUGH grant awards to each community’s lead organization for fiscal years 2025 through 2027. Source: Governor’s Office for Children grant records.
        The map
        Shaded areas are the census tracts each community serves. Markers show approximately where each community is. Boundaries: U.S. Census Bureau cartographic boundary files, 2024.
